# Declaration of Independence

- **Stable identifier:** `ag://gov.us.declaration-of-independence`
- **Title:** Declaration of Independence of the United States of America
- **Type:** Foundational government document
- **Adopted:** July 4, 1776
- **Engrossed on parchment:** Ordered July 19, 1776; signing began August 2, 1776
- **Collection:** `public-domain/`
- **Format:** `.guide`
- **Copyright status:** Public domain
- **Primary source:** U.S. National Archives, *Declaration of Independence: A Transcription*
- **Primary-source note:** The National Archives transcription is of the Stone Engraving of the parchment Declaration and states that its spelling and punctuation reflect the original.
- **Historical source:** U.S. National Archives, *Declaration of Independence (1776)*
- **Contents:** Declaration text and list of signers
- **Preservation note:** This edition preserves the historical wording rather than modernizing spelling, capitalization, or punctuation.
- **Conversion note:** The document is organized into AmigaGuide nodes for the declaration and signers, with a stable `ag://` library identifier for future library integration.
