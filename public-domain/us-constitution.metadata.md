# United States Constitution — Library Metadata

- **ID:** `ag://gov.us.constitution`
- **Title:** Constitution of the United States of America
- **Type:** Foundational government document
- **Original date:** September 17, 1787
- **Library collection:** `public-domain/`
- **Format:** AmigaGuide (`.guide`)
- **Source:** U.S. National Archives, *The Constitution of the United States: A Transcription*
- **Secondary verification:** U.S. Congress / Constitution Annotated, *Literal Print of the Constitution of the United States of America*
- **Original source status:** Public-domain historical government document
- **Contents:** Original Constitution, twenty-seven ratified amendments, and a list of the original signers
- **Preservation note:** The original Constitution is presented as historical text; amendments are presented separately rather than silently rewriting the 1787 text. Amendment XVIII is identified as repealed by Amendment XXI.
- **Conversion note:** Structure was organized into AmigaGuide nodes for the Preamble, Articles I–VII, amendments, and signers. Navigation links use AmigaGuide node links and the document carries the stable library identifier above.

The National Archives notes that its transcription reflects the spelling and punctuation of the Constitution as inscribed by Jacob Shallus on parchment. The U.S. Congress literal-print edition likewise states that its constitutional text reflects the National Archives high-resolution source material.
